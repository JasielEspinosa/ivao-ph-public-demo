Welcome to IVAO Philippines

IVAO Philippines is the home of virtual pilots and air traffic controllers who fly and control in Philippine airspace on the IVAO network. Founded in 2026, our community brings together flight simulation enthusiasts, real-world pilots and aspiring aviators who share a love for the Philippines and its busy skies over the Manila FIR.

Whether you want to fly from Manila to Cebu or sit in the controller chair at Manila Control, you will find friendly people, realistic procedures, training and regular events here. Create your IVAO account, join the community and take off with us.
